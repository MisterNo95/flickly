Neo-noir • 128 min • 2024

Midnight Overdrive

Directed by Ava Lin • Reviewed by Reel Review House

Score 4.8 / 5

Opening shot

Rain-slicked asphalt, a low synth note, and a camera glide that whispers, “stay in the dark.” The opening minutes establish the stakes with confident visual storytelling and a score that sticks like neon on a windshield.

What works

  • Lighting design paints the city in deep ruby tones, making every alley feel alive.
  • A lead performance that balances quiet menace with flickers of vulnerability.
  • Sound design pulses with rhythm, giving chase sequences a musical heartbeat.

Where it stumbles

The midsection lingers just a touch too long on exposition, but it never loses the tone. The payoff is worth the slow burn.

Final verdict

Midnight Overdrive is a lean, stylish noir with a modern heartbeat. It’s a theatre-seat must, especially for fans of neon-drenched thrillers.
