History of Tarot

Tarot began as a deck of playing cards in 15th-century Europe and evolved into an esoteric system through Renaissance mysticism and modern spiritual movements.

Origins

Italian courts commissioned lavish decks to celebrate noble houses.

Esoteric Revival

Occult scholars linked tarot to astrology, alchemy, and sacred geometry.

Modern Practice

Today tarot is a contemplative tool for personal and collective insight.
