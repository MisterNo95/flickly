Major & Minor Arcana

The Major Arcana reveals the soul journey, while the Minor Arcana reflects daily life and elemental lessons.

Major Arcana

22 archetypes including The Fool, The Star, and The World.

  • Initiation and destiny
  • Spiritual trials
  • Cosmic completion

Minor Arcana

Four suits: Cups, Wands, Swords, and Pentacles.

  • Cups: emotion
  • Wands: inspiration
  • Swords: intellect
  • Pentacles: material reality
